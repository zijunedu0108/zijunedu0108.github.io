💧 示例：Ways to save water at home 参考范例

Candidate A · Saving water while cooking
  • Washing vegetables in a bowl rather than under a running tap.
  • Reusing vegetable-washing water to rinse dirty pots.
主要是通过改变厨房用水的习惯（如用盆盛水洗菜、二次利用煮菜/洗菜水），减少洗涤时的无谓流水浪费。
Candidate B · Saving water while bathing
  • Taking shorter showers instead of full baths.
  • Turning off the tap while applying soap or shampoo.
聚焦于个人卫生习惯，通过控制淋浴时间和在搓抹沐浴露时关掉水龙头来大幅节约水量。
Candidate C · Saving water while gardening
  • Watering plants during early mornings or evenings to reduce evaporation.
  • Installing rainwater harvesting systems for garden irrigation.
针对户外园艺，利用选择蒸发量低的时段浇花，或收集雨水来代替自来水进行浇灌。
Candidate D · Saving water while cleaning
  • Using a bucket and mop instead of a continuous running hose.
  • Repurposing laundry greywater for scrubbing floors or outdoor tiles.
针对居家打扫，用“桶装水打扫”代替“水管冲洗”，并将洗衣后的废冲洗水二次用于打扫地面。
推荐谚语： “Waste not, want not.” (不浪费，不匮乏。)
