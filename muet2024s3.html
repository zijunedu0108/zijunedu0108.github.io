Booklet 1: Useful steps students can take to be successful in their studies

学生取得学业成功的有用步骤
Candidate A · Managing their time well
  • P1Prioritizing daily revision tasks through structured study schedules to avoid last-minute stress.
  • P2Balancing academic commitments with rest to maintain high productivity throughout the term.
通过制定有条理的学习时间表来合理安排复习优先顺序，避免临考压力，并在课业与休息之间取得平衡以保持高效。
Proverb Time and tide wait for no man. (岁月不等人 / 善于管理时间是成功的前提。)
Candidate B · Reading a lot
  • P1Broadening subject knowledge and vocabulary by reading beyond prescribed textbooks.
  • P2Enhancing critical thinking and comprehension skills needed for complex exam analysis.
广泛阅读课外书籍能拓展学科知识面和词汇量，同时提升应对复杂考试题型所需的批判性思维与理解能力。
Proverb Reading makes a full man. (读书使人充实。)
Candidate C · Doing past year question papers
  • P1Familiarizing oneself with exam formats, recurring question styles, and marking rubrics.
  • P2Improving time-management skills under simulated exam conditions to boost confidence.
刷历年真题能帮助熟悉题型与评分标准，并在模拟计时练习中掌握答题节奏、增强自信。
Proverb Practice makes perfect. (熟能生巧。)
Candidate D · Taking care of their health
  • P1Maintaining adequate sleep and a balanced diet to keep the brain focused and alert.
  • P2Preventing physical exhaustion and mental burnout during intense study periods.
保持充足的睡眠和均衡的饮食能让大脑时刻处于专注状态，防止在高强度复习期间出现身心透支。
Proverb A sound mind in a sound body. (健全的精神寓于健全的身体。)

Booklet 2: Which area of cleanliness should we focus on?

日常生活中应专注的卫生领域
Candidate A · Our home
  • P1Disinfecting living spaces and common surfaces regularly to eliminate dust, mold, and bacteria.
  • P2Creating a comfortable, clutter-free environment that reduces daily stress and promotes mental well-being.
定期消毒居住空间与公共表面能消除灰尘与细菌，同时打造干净整洁的家居环境以舒缓压力、促进心理健康。
Proverb East or west, home is best. (金窝银窝，不如自己的狗窝 / 家是最重要且需要呵护的港湾。)
Candidate B · Our clothes
  • P1Washing and drying garments thoroughly to prevent unpleasant body odors and skin infections.
  • P2Presenting a neat, well-groomed personal appearance to project professionalism and self-respect.
彻底清洗和晾晒衣服能防止体味与皮肤感染，同时保持整洁的外表也是尊重他人与建立良好第一印象的基础。
Proverb Clothes make the man. (人靠衣装 / 干净得体的穿搭体现个人素养。)
Candidate C · Our food
  • P1Washing fresh produce properly and cooking ingredients at safe temperatures to prevent food poisoning.
  • P2Storing raw and cooked items separately to avoid cross-contamination in the kitchen.
妥善清洗食材并用安全温度烹饪能预防食物中毒，将生熟食分开存放更能防止交叉污染。
Proverb You are what you eat. (人如其食 / 饮食卫生直接决定身体健康。)
Candidate D · Our body
  • P1Practicing daily personal hygiene like bathing and frequent handwashing to block disease transmission.
  • P2Protecting skin health and boosting individual immunity against external pathogens.
做好个人身体卫生（如勤洗手、洗澡）是阻断疾病传播的第一道防线，能有效保护皮肤与提升免疫力。
Proverb Cleanliness is next to godliness. (清洁乃圣洁之本 / 保持身体洁净是健康之基。)

Booklet 3: Ways to deal with hot weather

应对酷热天气的方法
Candidate A · Wearing suitable clothes
  • P1Choosing lightweight, loose-fitting, and light-colored cotton garments to allow better air circulation.
  • P2Reflecting sunlight and heat away from the body to keep cool outdoors.
穿著轻便、宽松且浅色的棉质衣服有助于空气流通，并能反射阳光热量以保持体表凉爽。
Proverb Cut your coat according to your cloth. (因地制宜 / 根据气候环境调整合适穿搭。)
Candidate B · Using fans and air conditioners
  • P1Regulating indoor temperatures and maintaining steady airflow during heatwaves.
  • P2Preventing heat-related illnesses such as heatstroke or exhaustion while resting indoors.
利用风扇与空调调节室内温度并保持空气循环，能在高温天气下预防中暑并保持居住舒适。
Proverb A quiet mind cures all heat. (心静自然凉 / 借助科技设备创造清凉环境。)
Candidate C · Drinking plenty of water
  • P1Replenishing fluid loss from continuous sweating to prevent severe dehydration.
  • P2Helping the body naturally regulate its internal core temperature.
补充因大量出汗所流失的水分以防脱水，同时协助身体维持正常的新陈代谢与体温调节。
Proverb Water is the driving force of all nature. (水是自然万物的源泉 / 补充水分是抵御酷热的核心。)
Candidate D · Avoiding outdoor activities
  • P1Minimizing direct exposure to intense ultraviolet (UV) radiation during peak sunny hours.
  • P2Reducing physical exertion in high temperatures to lower the risk of heat exhaustion.
在紫外线最强的时段减少室外活动，能有效避免阳光直射晒伤并降低高热下的体力消耗。
Proverb Discretion is the better part of valour. (明哲保身 / 避开酷暑高峰时段才是明智之举。)
